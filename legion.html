Note for the Legion front office

This is a working demo, not a mockup. The streak counter, the share block and the signup all run. Five questions instead of ten so it takes two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert here. On a live build it feeds your list, not mine.

Rugby has a problem no other local club has: plenty of people will come once, enjoy it, and never understand what they watched. Mixing two rules questions with three club questions each week turns that into something a newcomer can fix in two minutes, without anyone having to sit through an explainer video.

It also helps with the merger. A quiz is a painless way to carry San Diego's history into the new name while introducing the Los Angeles side of the club to fans here.

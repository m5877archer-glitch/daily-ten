Note for the Seals front office

This is a working demo, not a mockup. The streak counter, the share block and the signup all run. Five questions instead of ten so it takes two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert here. On a live build it feeds your list, not mine.

The timing is the reason I built this one. You are moving nine home games to a new building and migrating season ticket members with you. A weekly quiz is a low-effort way to keep those people in contact with the club through an off-season transition, and to introduce the Seals to North County families who have never been to a game.

Question five can carry whatever you need it to carry that week. Next opponent, a signing, a youth clinic, the Frontwave move itself.

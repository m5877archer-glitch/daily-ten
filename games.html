Games Worth Your Time
Ten questions takes about two minutes, which leaves most of your coffee unfinished. Here is where the rest of it goes. These are daily games worth your time, made by small independent outfits, most of them one or two people, all of them free to play in a browser.
GameWhat it is
Semantle Word
Guess the secret word by meaning instead of spelling. Brutally hard, and the guesses that get you there are half the fun.
Crosswordle Word
Two answers that cross, so every letter has to satisfy both. A tiny crossword wearing a Wordle coat.
Contexto Word
Unlimited guesses, ranked by how close you are. You will be at position 4,182 and absolutely certain you are onto something.
Mathler Numbers
Find the hidden equation that hits the target number. Wordle rules, arithmetic answers.
Sumplete Numbers
Delete numbers from a grid until every row and column adds up. Quieter than it looks.
putt.day Aim
One new hole of mini golf every day. Drag, release, groan. No words involved at all, which is sometimes the point.
Hey, Good Game Collection
A small studio that takes in daily games and keeps them running. Worth a browse when you want something you have not tried.
Make a daily game yourself? We are happy to add it. Write to hello@10everyday.com and tell us what it is and who made it. We link to people, not to clone farms, so the game should be yours.

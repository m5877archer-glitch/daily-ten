Note for the Michi team

This is a working demo, not a mockup. Everything here runs: the streak counter, the share block, the signup. Five questions instead of ten because a taproom quiz should take two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert in this demo. On a live version it feeds your list, not mine.

Questions are all Michi material and all public record, pulled from your taplist and the opening coverage. A stranger who has never been in can still reason through most of them, which is the point. Regulars get to feel like insiders, everyone else gets curious.

The dollar off a pour is a placeholder. Any perk works, or none. Happy to talk through what makes sense.

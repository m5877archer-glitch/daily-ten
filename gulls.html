Note for the Gulls front office

This is a working demo, not a mockup. The streak counter, the share block and the signup all run. Five questions instead of ten so it takes two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert here. On a live build it feeds your list, not mine.

An AHL club has a harder engagement problem than most teams, because the roster turns over constantly and casual fans cannot name five players. History solves that. The Gulls name goes back to 1966 in this city, and the current club just marked ten years in the AHL. That is a lot of material that does not expire when someone gets called up to Anaheim.

Question five can carry whatever you need it to carry that week. Next opponent, a theme night, a prospect worth watching.

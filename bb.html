Note for the Belching Beaver team

This is a working demo, not a mockup. The streak counter, the share block and the signup all run. Five questions instead of ten because it should take two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert here. On a live build it feeds your list, not mine.

One thing that separates you from a single taproom: your beer is already in a dozen states. A quiz that only works inside the tasting rooms wastes that. This one travels. A QR on a shelf talker or a case card puts it in front of someone standing in a grocery aisle in Phoenix, and the score block they paste into a group chat carries your name with it.

The Deftones question is the one I would build around. That collaboration gives you an audience that has nothing to do with craft beer and every reason to play a daily game with your logo on it.

The dollar off a pour is a placeholder. Any perk works, or none.

How it works

  • Everyone who plays is in. No signup needed.
  • Your first play of each edition counts, on the day it's published. Replays and back issues don't count.
  • Every weekday counts. A missed day is a zero, so showing up matters.
  • Highest total for the month wins. Ties go to whoever has more perfect 10s.
  • The prize is glory. The winner is named in the first edition of the next month.
  • You get a random name to start. Change it, or leave the contest, anytime below or on your score card.
  • Your scores are tied to this browser, so play on the same device all month.

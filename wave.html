Note for the Wave front office

This is a working demo, not a mockup. The streak counter, the share block and the signup all run. Five questions instead of ten so it takes two minutes, not five.

The signup form is deliberately inert here. On a live build it feeds your list, not mine.

The Wave's problem is not awareness, it is the gap between a 32,000-seat opener and an ordinary Wednesday. A weekly quiz is a small, cheap touch on the days nobody is playing, and it gives a family that came once a reason to learn the roster before they come back.

Question five can carry whatever you need it to carry that week. Next opponent, a signing, a ticket promotion, a community event.

It costs your customers two minutes and it puts you in front of them on the days they had no reason to think about you.

Every version below is built the same way and to the same standard. What changes is how often the questions are new, and that is the only thing that changes.

How this compares to a trivia night

A weeknight trivia host runs $150 to $250 for one evening, fills a few tables, and leaves. Weekly here is $300 for a whole month of it, reaches people who were never going to drive out on a Tuesday, and hands you an email list at the end of it.
